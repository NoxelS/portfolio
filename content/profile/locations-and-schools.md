Noel spent his early school years in Hubersdorf, Switzerland, from 2006 to 2014.

From 2014 to 2020, he attended IGS Nieder-Olm in Rheinland-Pfalz, where he completed both secondary education and the general higher education entrance qualification.

From 2020 to 2024, he studied physics at Saarland University. His planned next academic step is a master's degree in Padua beginning in 2026.
