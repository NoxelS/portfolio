## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies store small amounts of information in your browser. Technical cookies support a requested function or necessary website operation. Session storage holds information for the current browser tab.

### Language preference

When you select a language, the website sets the first-party cookie `portfolio_locale`. It stores your selected language, English or German, for up to one year.

Used solely to remember the language choice you request, this cookie does not require cookie consent. Associated personal-data processing, where applicable, is based on the legitimate interest in remembering that setting under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

You can delete the cookie in your browser settings. The website will then use its normal language selection again.

### Cloudflare cookies

Cloudflare Precursor processes browser and session interaction signals to distinguish automated traffic and protect the website. It uses the following cookies and browser storage:

| Name | Storage | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cf_clearance` | Cookie for noel.fyi and its subdomains. | Stores security-verification results and session state. | Up to one year; may be renewed during use. |
| `__cf_ob` | Cookie on the visited website hostname, restricted to `/cdn-cgi/challenge-platform`. | Supports Precursor's security verification and session state. | Browser session. |
| `_cfPre_tabId` | Browser session storage (`sessionStorage`). | Identifies the browser tab for security verification. | Tab session. |

Cloudflare's [Precursor documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-challenges/precursor/) explains its continuous security verification. Cookies and session storage can be cleared through your browser settings.

When Cloudflare's Always Online feature is used, the following short-lived cookies may be set:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cf_ob_info` | Stores origin-error details for Always Online. | 30 seconds |
| `cf_use_ob` | Directs requests to the Always Online cache. | 30 seconds |

These cookies support website availability. Cloudflare's [cookie reference](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/) explains these and other cookies associated with its delivery and protection features.

## Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure or restriction of processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to [contact@noel.fyi](mailto:contact@noel.fyi). If necessary, I may ask for proportionate additional information to verify your identity. Requests are generally answered within one month; if a permitted extension is needed, I will explain it within that period.

You may complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/), or to the competent supervisory authority in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the alleged infringement.

### Your right to object

You may object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests. I will stop that processing unless compelling legitimate grounds override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the information is needed for legal claims.

Contact [contact@noel.fyi](mailto:contact@noel.fyi) to exercise this right.
